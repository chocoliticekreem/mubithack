**Patterns [O]:**
1. **The formula is familiar plus one twist.** Dead Rails (Build A Boat plus zombies), Steal An Egg (Steal a Brainrot plus eggs). For us, it's Natural Disaster Survival plus Overcooked roles plus firefighting.
2. **Collectible cute creatures** show up in Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, Steal An Egg and 99 Nights. For us, that's the rescued pets, with **the panda as the rare one**.
3. **Scheduled live events** like admin abuse and weekly updates drive concurrent-player spikes far more than the base game does.
4. **Big Roblox YouTubers and TikTok** decide what breaks out. The game has to *look* funny in a 15-second clip with no sound.

## 3. What makes co-op chaos games viral on TikTok and YouTube

Sourced foundation: Lethal Company's virality came from proximity voice and panicked reactions [S] (Yahoo).
Friendslop works because it is low-friction, social-first and shareable as clips, and because a group buys
together [S] (AppMagic, Inverse). Chained Together grew through streamers blaming each other [S] (Streams Charts).
Roblox's own Moments feed gives players **30-second in-game clips that viewers can tap to join the game** [S]
([Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/07/moments-new-homepage-unlocks-gaming-for-all), [Deltia's](https://deltiasgaming.com/what-is-roblox-moments-new-clips-feature-explained/)).

What makes a moment clip-able [O]:
- **The betrayal beat:** a friend hoses a wet floor next to the fuse box, and the teammate fries with a skeleton flash and a ragdoll. The caption writes itself: *"he did it on purpose"*.
- **The rescue-gone-wrong:** carrying the panda out of a burning house, someone sprays you, and you drop it into the pool.
- **The wrong tool:** watering a flower while the room burns, which is straight out of the trailer.
- **The overkill fix:** blowing up the dam so a tidal wave floods the whole town. It's a spectacle you can screenshot.
- **The blame screen:** an end-of-round awards card ("🔥 Arsonist of the round: Player 2") is a ready-made, shareable verdict. **This is the single most important viral asset.** It turns 3 minutes of chaos into one screenshot with a punchline and names a friend to tag.
- **Readable without sound:** big googly eyes, soot-black faces and numbered helmets mean the joke works on a muted feed.
- **Ragdoll physics** are funny on any device.

## 4. Roblox-specific retention hooks

Platform facts [S]:
- Roblox's "Recommended For You" ranking moved (Jun 2026) to a **28-day retention view in three windows (Day 1, Days 2–7, Days 8–28)**. It counts playtime, play days, *intentional co-play days*, spend days and Robux [S?] ([zehn-studio26](https://zehn-studio26.com/news/recommended-for-you-retention-update/), [DevForum on RFY](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441)). **Playing with friends is now an explicit ranking signal.**
- Roblox has a **friend referral system** where creators can track invites and reward them [S?] (TikTok coverage).
- Users aged 13+ were 64% of daily active users in Q2 2025 and were the fastest-growing group [S] ([Roblox Q2'25 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509825000261/q225shletterex992.htm)).

Hooks for Flame and Blame [O]:
| Hook | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **Short sessions** | 3-minute callouts, so one more round is always cheap. Mobile-friendly. |
| **Join-in-progress** | Drop-in friends join at the station. Server size of **4–8**: lobby up to 8, squads of 4 per truck. |
| **Party = progression** | A squad XP bonus for playing with friends (a co-play signal for the ranking) plus referral rewards (a "Rookie badge" helmet for both players). |
| **Collection** | A rescued-pet shelter at the station: every pet you save lives there. Common dog and cat, rare panda, a "legendary" flaming chicken. This borrows the Grow a Garden and Steal An Egg collection drive. |
| **Progression** | Station rank, tool upgrades (bigger tank, faster axe), new districts (suburb, then school, then forest, then dam). |
| **Blame stats** | Lifetime "times electrocuted a teammate" on your profile card. Funny leaderboards beat skill leaderboards. |
| **Live events** | A weekly "City-wide Blaze" (admin-style event) with a limited helmet. |
| **Daily** | A daily "callout of the day" modifier: low water, wind, or a pet-only mission. |

## 5. Monetization ideas for Roblox

The platform supports Game Passes (permanent), Developer Products (consumables), Premium payouts and **Rewarded Video ads**
(opt-in videos up to 30 seconds for an in-game reward, with **>80% average completion** in tests) [S] ([Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/04/roblox-scales-video-ads-partners-with-google)).
DevEx pays **$0.0038 per Robux** since 5 Sep 2025 [S] ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/increasing-devex-%E2%80%94-creators-will-now-earn-85-more/3920159)).
Creators earned $1.5B in 2025 [S] ([Roblox 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)).

Ideas, cosmetics-first and never pay-to-win against friends [O]:
- **Cosmetics:** helmet styles and numbers, sirens, truck paint jobs, hose water colours (rainbow water, a nod to the trailer), funny death effects such as a cartoon skeleton or a soot puff.
- **Game Passes:** a VIP fire chief truck with a megaphone, a pet-shelter extension, and a "Blame Immunity" title. (It's cosmetic only and only changes the award wording.)
- **Developer Products:** pet eggs or crates for station pets, and a revive token.
- **Rewarded video:** watch an ad to double end-of-round coins or refill the water tank instantly once per round.
- **Private servers** for friend squads and streamers.
- **Avoid:** selling power that hurts teammates, and loot boxes aimed at under-13 players.

## 6. One-paragraph pitch

**Flame and Blame (Roblox):** the alarm rings, and you and up to three friends sprint to the truck as the
worst firefighters in town. In 3-minute callouts you hose down spreading fires, axe through locked doors and
carry trapped civilians, dogs, cats and one very confused panda back to the truck. Meanwhile your best friend
sprays a wet floor next to a fuse box and electrocutes you. Every round ends with the **Blame Board**, which
names the hero, the arsonist and the panda saver in a screenshot made for TikTok. It takes the proven
firefighting theme from Roblox roleplay, the cute-rescue drive of 99 Nights, and the friendslop chaos that
took over Steam, and puts them in a short, friend-first game that the Roblox algorithm now rewards. And Forge
built it from a single sentence, then made it better every time a playtester or a judge corrected it.

## 7. Five moments the demo should show

1. **The alarm and the scramble:** the bell rings, the round timer starts, and players run from the station to the burning block. It reads as a game in 3 seconds.
2. **Fire spreads and the hose beats it back:** a part ignites its neighbours, the hose sprays, flames shrink, and the water tank drains and refills at the hydrant. This is the satisfying PowerWash moment that proves the systems are real and not faked.
3. **The axe and the panda:** an axe breaks a locked door, the panda is inside, and someone carries it back to the truck. The rescue counter ticks up. This is the emotional beat.
4. **The friendly-fire gag:** water plus the fuse box electrocutes a teammate (or a fire jumps onto a teammate) with a ragdoll and a sound. If v2 isn't ready, use any funny failure that ForgeTelemetry counts. This is the clip moment.
5. **The Blame Board, then the Forge correction:** the results screen hands out awards. Then show the Mubit beat: a judge or teammate says something like "the panda is too easy to find" or "fire spreads too fast", Forge applies the correction, the next playtest visibly changes, and the counters move (`fires_spread` drops, `rescued` rises). **This is the moment that wins the "use of Mubit" score**, because it shows a correction turning into a model update, as the hackathon rules require.
