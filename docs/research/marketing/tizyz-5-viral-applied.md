# Tizyz: "5 things that guarantee a viral Roblox game", applied to Be a Firefighter

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FnsIL4zPCQg (transcript: `FnsIL4zPCQg.txt`).

| # | His rule | Where we are | What we do next |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Clickable core fantasy.** What a 7-year-old imagines trying to fall asleep. | "What if I was a firefighter: drive the truck, spray the fire, save the kid / panda." Strong and universal. Name, icon and ads now say it in 3rd-grade words. Best ad CTRs are single, clear moments ("SAVE THE PANDA!" 2.0%, "PUT OUT THE FIRE!" 1.8%). | Keep every creative to one fantasy moment. Show the trapped panda/kid, the hose stream and the big red truck. |
| 2 | **Onboarding = emotional investment in the first 90 s**, not a list of instructions. One mechanic at a time, minimal UI, cutscenes cut bounce. Diagnose with Engagement → *new user first-session retention* (30 s / 1 min / 5 min) and a funnel with every tiny step, sliced by device. | The first thing a player does is a calm training fire with an extinguisher. Nothing is at stake, and the full HUD (XP, PASS, STORE, NEWS, ADMIN) is on screen from second 1. Our funnel is coarse (8 steps) and not split by device. | **Cold open:** a 6–8 s cutscene of a house on fire, a panda in the window shouting HELP, and the truck siren. Then "SAVE THE PANDA!" and the first spray. **Hide the HUD** until the first call is done, and reveal it one piece at a time. **Finer funnel:** every step, tagged by device (phone / tablet / PC / console). |
| 3 | **Socialization:** 10× more fun with friends, so friends invite friends. Leaderboards, visible flex, other players zooming past you. | Squads of up to 8, one truck per squad, invite chip, pass perk badges. But nothing in the core loop *needs* or rewards a second player, and there's no leaderboard in the lobby. | **Lobby hero board** (today's top rescuers / fires out) on the station wall. **Team bonuses:** two hoses on one fire = COMBO and faster knock-down, a two-person carry is faster, and the squad streak shows on the results screen. **Show other squads' trucks** leaving with sirens. |
| 4 | **Simple mechanic, tons of depth**, and it must be easy on mobile. Every session should feel different. | Spray, chop, carry: simple. Depth comes from 15 sites, interiors, random events and fire spread. Mobile driving, spraying and tap-to-use were reworked this week. | Make every call visibly different: a random twist per call (cat on the roof, gas leak, blocked road), plus a "how fast can we do it" squad time on the results screen. |
| 5 | **Clippable / strong community.** Picture a content creator playing: what moments get clipped? | The Blame system (scapegoat award, slapping, throwing) and backdrafts are naturally funny. Nothing frames these moments or makes them shareable. | **Big set pieces**: backdraft blast, roof collapse, the truck honking through a crowd. Add a 2-second slow-mo plus an on-screen caption on the funniest blame moments, and a results-screen "BLAME OF THE CALL" card worth screenshotting. |

## Order

1. Onboarding v3 (cold open, hidden HUD, per-device funnel). This is the biggest lever on bounce and session time.
2. Social: hero board plus team bonuses.
3. Clippable moments.
4. Per-call twists.

Check **Engagement → New user first-session retention** once the Oct 24–25 analytics land (Roblox lags 1–2 days). His bar is about 95% at 30 s, 87% at 1 min and 65% at 5 min.
