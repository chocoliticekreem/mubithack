# Ad and event copy: [UPD] Be a Firefighter 🚒 (Station 67)

Short lines for the Roblox text fields: the experience description, Experience Events, update notes and
campaign names. The playbook rules apply:
- Write at a 3rd-grade reading level, verb + noun ("Save the puppy").
- Sell the core fantasy (be a firefighter) and hint at a challenge.
- Keep claims honest: no "best", "#1", "free", Robux, prices, "911" or racing.
- Use only these emoji, because they have been around for years and Roblox accepts them:
  🔥 🚒 💧 🐶 🐱 🐼 📻 ⏰ 🌙 ⭐ 🎉 👋 🏠 🚨.
- Never use newer emoji like 🧯 or 🟩, because Roblox rejects them.

## Title (keep as is)
`[UPD] Be a Firefighter 🚒`

```
🚒 NEW: everyone gets a fire truck.
🌙 A new map every server: snow, fog, sunset and night.
⏰ Beat the clock on every call.
```

## Words we don't use
- **"911"**: in-game it's dispatch.
- **"race"**: there's no racing.
- **"best", "#1", "most popular"**: thumbnail rules ban claims.
- **"free"** and **"Robux"**.
- **"only 1%" style stats**: we have no data to back them up.
- **"60 seconds"**: on-scene timers run for minutes, so we say "beat the clock".
